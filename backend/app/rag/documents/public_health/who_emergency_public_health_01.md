## Chapter 1: Water Treatment, Disinfection & Waterborne Disease Outbreak Control
**Section:** Water Treatment, Disinfection & Waterborne Disease Outbreak Control | **Page Reference:** 16

Preventing post-disaster outbreaks of Cholera, Typhoid, and Hepatitis A:
1. Shock Chlorination of Open Wells: Dose municipal wells with 50g of bleaching powder per 1,000 liters of water. Allow 1-hour contact time before distribution.
2. Free Residual Chlorine (FRC) Testing: Maintain FRC at point of delivery at minimum 0.5 mg/L in normal periods and 1.0 mg/L during active cholera / diarrhea outbreaks.
3. Syndromic Surveillance: Establish daily surveillance reporting for Acute Diarrheal Disease (ADD), Acute Respiratory Infections (ARI), and unexplained fevers.
4. Isolation Tents: Erect dedicated quarantine treatment centers with independent latrines immediately upon detection of more than 2 clustered cholera or dysentery cases.
