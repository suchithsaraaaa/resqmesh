## Chapter 1: IMD 4-Stage Cyclone Warning Stages & Emergency Actions
**Section:** IMD 4-Stage Cyclone Warning Stages & Emergency Actions | **Page Reference:** 8

India Meteorological Department (IMD) cyclone alerts mandate progressive operational actions:
1. Stage 1 — Pre-Cyclone Watch (72h prior): Initiate multi-agency command meetings, verify coastal cyclone shelter readiness, test HAM radio and satellite communication terminals.
2. Stage 2 — Cyclone Alert (48h prior): Enforce total suspension of fishing operations, recall all sea-going vessels, issue public sirens in coastal taluks.
3. Stage 3 — Cyclone Warning (24h prior): Execute mandatory phased evacuation of all thatch and kutchha house inhabitants within 5 km of coastline.
4. Stage 4 — Post-Landfall Outlook (12h prior): Finalize shelter lockdowns. Cease all outdoor vehicle movement. Prepare emergency clearance taskforces for immediate post-landfall deployment.

## Chapter 2: Storm Surge Inundation & Coastal Shelter Management
**Section:** Storm Surge Inundation & Coastal Shelter Management | **Page Reference:** 24

Storm surges accompanying severe cyclonic storms represent the highest coastal mortality risk:
1. Vertical Evacuation: Relocate populations living below 10m elevation to Multi-purpose Cyclone Shelters (MPCS) engineered on stilted reinforced concrete pilings.
2. Window and Structural Shuttering: Secure shelter windows with steel mesh shutters. High-velocity winds carrying corrugated tin sheets produce lethal airborne shrapnel.
3. Food and Power Self-Sufficiency: Maintain 72-hour autonomous emergency diesel generator fuel reserves and potable water stored in elevated indoor poly-tanks.
4. Communication Redundancy: When cellular towers are uprooted, activate VHF/UHF wireless networks and ResQMesh peer-to-peer relay nodes placed at high elevation.
