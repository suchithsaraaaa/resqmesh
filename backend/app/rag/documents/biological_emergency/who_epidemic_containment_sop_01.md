## Infectious Disease Outbreak Containment, Isolation Corridors & Quarantine Principles
*Reference Page: 12*

Operational guidelines for containment of acute biological outbreaks and respiratory epidemics:
1. Primary Isolation Architecture: Establish negative-pressure isolation rooms (minimum 12 air changes per hour, exhausted outdoors away from air intakes) for airborne pathogens. Where negative pressure is unavailable, place patients in well-ventilated single rooms with exhaust fans directing air away from hospital corridors.
2. Standard, Contact & Droplet Precautions: Enforce strict hand hygiene with 70% alcohol hand rub or soap and water for 30 seconds. Maintain 2-meter physical distance from symptomatic patients.
3. Quarantine Management: Contacts of confirmed index cases must complete 14-day monitored home or facility quarantine with daily symptom and temperature screening. Restrict visitor access strictly to essential caregivers.

## Biological PPE Donning/Doffing Sequences & Biohazard Waste Disinfection
*Reference Page: 28*

Strict personal protective equipment protocols for high-consequence biological hazards:
1. Donning Sequence: Hand hygiene -> Gown/Tyvek coverall -> N95/FFP3 respirator (perform user seal check: positive and negative pressure tests) -> Eye protection (goggles or face shield) -> Double nitrile examination gloves (inner glove under cuff, outer glove over cuff).
2. Doffing Sequence (Highest Risk of Self-Contamination): Clean gloved hands with sanitizer -> Remove outer gloves inside-out -> Remove gown pulling forward away from body -> Hand hygiene -> Remove goggles touching only head-strap -> Remove respirator touching only rear elastic straps (NEVER touch front of mask) -> Remove inner gloves -> Final alcohol hand rub.
3. Biohazard Waste Management: Double-bag all contaminated PPE and patient dressings in yellow clinical waste bags. Disinfect surfaces with freshly prepared 0.5% (5,000 ppm) sodium hypochlorite solution leaving surface wet for at least 10 minutes before wiping.
