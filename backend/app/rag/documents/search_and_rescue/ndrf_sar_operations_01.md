## FEMA / INSARAG Structural Search Marking System (X-Code Box)
*Reference Page: 22*

International standardized search marking for structures (2-foot by 2-foot spray-paint 'X' on entry wall):
1. Initial Slash (/): Painted upon entry. Indicates search team is currently inside the building.
2. Completing the Cross (X): Completed upon search team exit. Enter operational data into the four quadrants:
   - TOP QUADRANT: Date and time the search team exited (e.g. '29-AUG 14:30').
   - LEFT QUADRANT: Search team identifier (e.g. 'NDRF-BN10' or 'RESQMESH-ALPHA').
   - RIGHT QUADRANT: Critical hazards identified inside structure (e.g. 'RATS', 'GAS', 'ASBESTOS', 'UNSTABLE 2ND FLOOR').
   - BOTTOM QUADRANT: Number of victims found: Live (L) and Deceased (D) (e.g. '2L / 1D').
3. Confined Space Entry: Continuous forced-air ventilation is mandatory. Responders must wear full-body harnesses with retrieval winch line attached to an overhead tripod before entry.
