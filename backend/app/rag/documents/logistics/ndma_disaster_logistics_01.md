## Chapter 1: Emergency Resource Tracking, Incident Staging Areas & Supply Chain Management
**Section:** Emergency Resource Tracking, Incident Staging Areas & Supply Chain Management | **Page Reference:** 12

How emergency resources should be tracked and allocated during disaster response:
1. Strategic Staging Area Selection: Locate logistics base outside hazard zone but within 15 minutes transit time. Require hardstand parking for 50+ heavy trucks and helicopter landing zone (helipad).
2. FIFO Inventory Tracking: Implement First-In, First-Out inventory controls on perishable food, pharmaceuticals, and sterile medical consumables. Track emergency resources with digital manifests.
3. Dedicated Fuel Depots: Establish separate secure fuel storage for Diesel (fire tenders, boats, generators) and Aviation Turbine Fuel (ATF) with Class B foam fire protection.
4. Cold-Chain Integrity: Maintain continuous 2°C to 8°C temperature monitoring for insulin, blood products, and vaccines using solar-powered portable refrigeration units.
