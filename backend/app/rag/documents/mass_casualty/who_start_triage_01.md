## START Triage Protocol for Adult Casualties (30-2-Can Do)
*Reference Page: 10*

Simple Triage and Rapid Treatment (START) algorithm for adult mass casualty response (< 60 seconds per casualty):
1. Step 1 (Walking Wounded): Broadcast verbal instruction: 'Anyone who can hear my voice and walk, move to the designated green flag assembly point now'. All casualties who walk are categorized GREEN (Minor / Walking Wounded).
2. Step 2 (Respiration Assessment): Check breathing: If NOT breathing, open airway (chin lift/jaw thrust). If still NOT breathing after opening airway, categorize BLACK (Deceased / Expectant). If breathing resumes or is GREATER THAN 30 breaths/minute, categorize RED (Immediate / Critical). If breathing is 10 to 30 breaths/minute, move to Step 3.
3. Step 3 (Perfusion Assessment): Check radial pulse or capillary refill. If radial pulse is ABSENT or capillary refill > 2 seconds, categorize RED (Immediate). Control any active arterial bleeding immediately. If radial pulse is present, move to Step 4.
4. Step 4 (Mental Status Assessment): Assess ability to obey a simple command ('Squeeze my hand'). If unable to follow simple commands, categorize RED (Immediate). If able to follow simple commands, categorize YELLOW (Delayed / Urgent).

## JumpSTART Pediatric Triage for Infants & Children Under 8 Years
*Reference Page: 24*

JumpSTART pediatric triage protocol modified for children under 8 years of age:
1. Normal Pediatric Physiology: Children naturally have higher respiratory rates (normal 15-45 breaths/min). Do not categorize a child Red solely because breathing rate is > 30.
2. The 5 Rescue Breaths Exception: In children, cardiac arrest is almost always secondary to respiratory arrest. If child is apneic, open airway. If still apneic, check for peripheral pulse. If pulse is PRESENT, deliver 5 gentle rescue breaths. If breathing resumes, categorize RED (Immediate). If breathing does NOT resume after 5 rescue breaths, categorize BLACK (Deceased).
3. Respiratory Thresholds: If spontaneous breathing is < 15 or > 45 breaths/min, categorize RED. If between 15-45, check pulse.
4. Perfusion & Neurological (AVPU): If pulse is absent, categorize RED. For neurological check, use AVPU scale (Alert, Voice, Pain, Unresponsive). Inappropriate response to pain or unresponsive child = RED. Appropriate response = YELLOW.
