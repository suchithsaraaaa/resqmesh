## Chapter 1: Temporary Shelter Organization, SPHERE Living Space, Water & Sanitation Standards
**Section:** Temporary Shelter Organization, SPHERE Living Space, Water & Sanitation Standards | **Page Reference:** 16

How a temporary shelter should be organized during emergency disaster relief:
1. Covered Living Space: Minimum 3.5 square meters of covered living space per person in temporary shelters, excluding cooking and exterior circulation areas.
2. Potable Water Supply: Minimum 15 liters of safe drinking and domestic water per person per day. Maximum distance to nearest water point: 500 meters.
3. Sanitation & Toilet Ratios: Maximum 20 persons per latrine / toilet. Strictly segregate male and female toilets with secure interior locking doors and exterior lighting.
4. Vector Control and Waste Disposal: Provide 100-liter refuse containers with lids for every 10 family shelters; empty containers daily to prevent fly and rodent breeding.

## Chapter 2: Food Distribution, Nutrition & Vulnerable Groups
**Section:** Food Distribution, Nutrition & Vulnerable Groups | **Page Reference:** 35

Nutritional and medical relief protocols in relief settlements:
1. Caloric Intake: Ensure minimum daily intake of 2,100 kcal per person, including balanced micronutrients (iron, vitamin A, iodine).
2. Infant and Young Child Feeding: Protect exclusive breastfeeding. Strictly prohibit unmanaged public donations of infant formula / milk powders due to unsanitary water reconstitution risks.
3. Special Protection Corridors: Establish designated child-friendly spaces and women-only support spaces with round-the-clock female security personnel.
